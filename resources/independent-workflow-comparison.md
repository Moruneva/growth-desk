# Open research: compare your free workflow with Moruneva

**Independent, voluntary, unpaid product research — 8 November 2026.**

Moruneva is inviting people who actually handle creator-content usage reviews or n8n workflow handoffs to test a **fictional scenario**. We want to learn whether our free, browser-local tools add anything beyond methods already available at no cost. A spreadsheet, your own checklist, or the native workflow tool might be just as good.

**You do not need to create an account, pay, install software, or share customer files.** We do not claim an existing user community, successful trials, or any paid customers. This invitation is separate from Growth Desk, which has not been released.

## Option A — creator-content usage window (recommended)

**Who:** Someone who has actually checked whether a creator asset may be reused in an advertising schedule (for a brand, agency, or creator). You are welcome to say the task is already solved.

1. Read the [free spreadsheet-first checklist and three completely fictional cases](creator-rights-preflight.md).
2. **First**, use your normal free spreadsheet/manual method to review fictional Case A (paid ads scheduled through Dec 5 although the recorded date window ends Nov 30), Case B (creator-handle ad authorization needs separate platform verification), and Case C (organic reuse recorded but paid advertising proposed). Record minutes spent and which items remain uncertain.
3. **Then**, open the free [Moruneva Creator Rights Window Checker](https://moruneva.com/creator-rights-window), choose **Load three fictional cases**, and review the same cases. Again record minutes, useful flags, mistaken or omitted flags, and anything that still needs a person to check.
4. Compare whether the checker saved any time or caught something your existing free method did not. A finding of **no added value** is equally useful. No need to send any contract, clip, creator name, platform credential, or screenshot.

The checker offers planning prompts only. It does **not** determine contract validity, licensing rights, or platform authorization.

## Option B — n8n credential-reference handoff

**Who:** Someone who has actually handed off or taken over an n8n workflow.

1. Open the free [Moruneva Credential Handoff Map](https://moruneva.com/n8n-credential-handoff-map) and download the **completely fictional four-node example** shown there. Do not use your real workflow.
2. **First**, inspect the sample using your ordinary free method, such as a manual workflow review or native n8n tools. Note the credential types and node positions that may require human setup; record elapsed minutes and remaining unknowns.
3. **Then**, inspect the *same fictional sample* with the Moruneva browser-local checker. Record the same items and time.
4. Note differences, missed references, false confidence, and whether a handoff like this comes up often enough that an improved process would matter.

The checker does **not** verify live access, credential validity, scopes, or successful transfer. Do not enter any real API keys or customer data. You are **not required to import or run** the fictional sample in n8n.

## Optional feedback (about five short answers)

Send only your own observations, without any customer details:

1. Which track did you test, and what kind of work do you do (general role only)?
2. Time using the existing free method: `___` min; using Moruneva: `___` min. Was the comparison fair?
3. What did either method miss or leave uncertain?
4. Would you choose the free method, Moruneva, or neither for the same task next week? Why?
5. If the task repeats in your work, which **specific additional outcome**, if any, would be worth paying for? “Nothing” is a valid answer.
